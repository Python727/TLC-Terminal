

You will need a modloader, either DML (DmgModLoader) or UE4SS.
Unpack the zip content into ..\Voyage\Voyage\Content\Paks\LogicMods

For DML
open the console prompt in-game (double tapping ~) and type dml add TerminalWaypoint, and reload save to apply changes.
For UE4SS
It's automatic, but this tool is heavy on performance and may crash randomly.

Notes
Save pull delay is 10 seconds (can change, and it's also configurable).
